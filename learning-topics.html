FROM KNOWLEDGE TO CONTRIBUTION

Learning Should Lead Somewhere

GHE is designed not only to help young people learn about global health, but also to help them understand their place within a changing world.

Through exploration, reflection, evidence, creativity, and action, learners can develop the confidence and skills to contribute to healthier communities.

The learning pathways therefore connect naturally with the broader GHE journey—from curiosity and knowledge to meaningful participation and leadership.

01 Curiosity Ask questions about the world.
02 Knowledge Build understanding through learning.
03 Reflection Examine evidence and perspectives.
04 Action Apply learning to real-world challenges.
05 Leadership Help create positive change.
