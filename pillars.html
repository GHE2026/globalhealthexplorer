THE FOUNDATION OF GHE

Five Pillars

The principles that guide how Global Health Explorers learns, acts, grows, and serves future generations.

FROM PRINCIPLES TO PRACTICE

What Guides Global Health Explorers?

The Constitution of Global Health Explorers establishes the organization's purpose, values, and long-term commitment. The Five Pillars translate those principles into a practical framework for learning, action, leadership, and institutional development.

Together, the pillars provide a common foundation for learners, educators, families, mentors, partners, and future leaders who contribute to the GHE community.

01

Learning

GHE places learning at the heart of its mission. Young people are encouraged to explore global health, science, society, environment, equity, and human wellbeing through accessible and engaging education.

Learning is understood as a continuous process that develops knowledge, curiosity, critical thinking, and the confidence to ask meaningful questions.

02

Equity

GHE recognizes that health and opportunity are not distributed equally. Understanding inequities and their underlying determinants is essential to advancing healthier communities.

Equity therefore informs how GHE approaches education, participation, partnerships, technology, and opportunities for young people.

03

Evidence & Innovation

GHE encourages learners to value evidence, scientific inquiry, data literacy, creativity, and responsible innovation.

New technologies, including artificial intelligence, should be explored thoughtfully, with attention to accuracy, ethics, transparency, and potential impact.

04

Compassion & Community

Global health is ultimately about people. GHE encourages empathy, respect, compassion, and a sense of responsibility toward others and the communities in which we live.

Learners are encouraged to listen to diverse perspectives, appreciate different experiences, and understand that meaningful health improvement depends on collaboration and collective action.

05

Leadership & Action

GHE believes that knowledge becomes most meaningful when it can contribute to positive action. Young people are encouraged to transform learning and reflection into constructive contributions.

Leadership is understood not simply as authority, but as the ability to listen, collaborate, take responsibility, inspire others, and contribute to healthier and more equitable communities.

Five Pillars. One Shared Purpose.

The Five Pillars are interconnected rather than independent. Together, they create a framework in which knowledge is connected with equity, evidence with responsibility, compassion with community, and learning with meaningful action.

FROM VALUES TO ACTION

How the Pillars Shape GHE

Education

Learning experiences are designed to develop knowledge, curiosity, critical thinking, empathy, and responsible citizenship.

Innovation

GHE encourages young people to use science, technology, data, and creativity to explore meaningful challenges.

Participation

Learners are encouraged to contribute ideas, collaborate with others, and become active members of their communities.

Leadership

GHE creates pathways through which learning and participation can develop into leadership and sustained contribution.

PILLARS IN PRACTICE

From Principles to Programs

The Five Pillars provide a common foundation across the GHE ecosystem. They are reflected in the way learning is designed, challenges are developed, young people participate, and partnerships are built.

Education Framework

The Education Framework translates the pillars into a progressive learning experience that develops knowledge, reflection, action, recognition, and leadership.

Global Health Big Data Challenge

GHBDC brings evidence, data literacy, innovation, equity, and responsible leadership together through real-world global health questions.

Young Voices

GHE creates opportunities for young people to express ideas, share perspectives, develop confidence, and contribute to conversations about healthier communities.

Community & Partnerships

GHE connects learners with educators, families, researchers, institutions, and communities to strengthen learning and collective action.

CONTINUOUS LEARNING

The Pillars Grow with GHE

The Five Pillars are intended to provide continuity while allowing GHE to learn, adapt, and improve. As global health challenges, technologies, educational approaches, and the needs of young people evolve, GHE will continue to reflect on its experience and strengthen its programs.

This commitment to continuous learning and improvement ensures that the principles of GHE remain meaningful not only today, but for future generations.

OUR COMMITMENT

Principles That Guide Us

The Five Pillars are grounded in the Constitution of Global Health Explorers and serve as a living framework for translating its purpose and values into practice.

Together, they guide GHE toward a future in which young people are equipped not only with knowledge, but also with the values, skills, confidence, and sense of responsibility needed to contribute to a healthier and more equitable world.
