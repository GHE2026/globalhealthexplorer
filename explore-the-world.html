Every difference matters. Explore it.
Why Explore the World?

The world is not one average.

Countries are different. Communities within countries are different. People experience health and wellbeing in different ways.

🌍

Countries are different

Geography, history, economies, environments and health systems shape different experiences.

👥

Communities are different

National averages can hide important differences between communities and population groups.

📊

Data reveal patterns

Good data can help us see differences that are difficult to see from individual stories alone.

🔎

Data need context

A number has meaning only when we understand what was measured, for whom, where, when and how.

A number is not the answer. It is the beginning of a question.

The GHE Data Rule

Before you trust a number, understand the number.

CHECK THE WHOLE INDICATOR
Indicator → Definition → Numerator / Denominator → Population → Place → Time → Source → Uncertainty → Context
A value can be correct for its definition and still be inappropriate for the comparison you want to make. Always check the metadata.
Trusted Data Gateways

Explore real global data

GHE distinguishes authoritative public-health information from educational and data-exploration resources.

WHO

WHO Global Health Observatory

Explore global health indicators across Member States, including mortality, disease, risk factors, immunization, environmental health, health systems and other areas.

Explore WHO GHO ↗
HIDR

WHO Health Inequality Monitor

Explore disaggregated health data and investigate measurable differences across population groups, places and other dimensions.

Health Inequality Monitor ↗ WHO HEAT ↗
IHME

Global Burden of Disease

Explore estimates of disease burden, causes, risks and health outcomes across locations, populations and time.

Explore IHME GBD ↗
UNICEF

UNICEF Data

Particularly useful for exploring children and adolescents through health, nutrition, education, WASH, protection and other indicators.

Explore UNICEF Data ↗
WB

World Bank Data

Explore internationally comparable indicators related to development, health, education, environment, gender and economies.

Explore World Bank Data ↗
OWID

Our World in Data

A powerful learning and visualization resource bringing together data from many providers with clear charts and explanations.

Explore OWID ↗
Remember: the purpose of a data source matters. Official public-health information and educational visualization resources should not be treated as interchangeable.
