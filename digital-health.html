Digital Health

Your digital world can influence your health, your choices and the way you connect with health information and services. Explore digital wellbeing, health data, privacy, AI, telehealth, equity and the future of global health.

WHY DOES THIS MATTER?

Why is Digital Health important?

Digital technology is now part of everyday life. We use phones, computers, websites, apps and connected devices to learn, communicate, exercise, find information and increasingly interact with health services.

But technology does not automatically make health better. A digital tool can be useful, misleading, inaccessible, unsafe or unfair depending on how it is designed and used.

GHE QUESTION

How can we use digital technology to improve health while protecting people, communities and equity?

GLOBAL HEALTH AT A GLANCE

Digital Health is bigger than screen time

2027

AI

EQUITY

TRUST

LEARN

Digital Wellbeing & Screen Time

Digital wellbeing is not simply a question of counting minutes on a screen.

What matters includes what we are doing, why we are doing it, what it replaces, how it affects sleep and activity, and whether technology supports or interferes with our relationships, learning and wellbeing.

Personal Hints

  • Notice how technology affects your sleep and concentration.
  • Protect time for movement, learning, relationships and rest.
  • Take intentional breaks when they help you refocus.
  • Ask whether a digital activity is helping you achieve what you intended.

THINK CRITICALLY

Health Misinformation & Disinformation

A message can be popular without being true. A confident claim is not automatically good evidence.

Misinformation

Incorrect information shared without necessarily intending to cause harm.

Disinformation

False or misleading information deliberately created or spread to deceive or influence.

YOUR DATA

Health Data & Your Digital Footprint

What is health data?

Health data can include information about health conditions, measurements, activity, appointments, medications and other characteristics relevant to health.

What is a digital footprint?

Your digital footprint is the trail of information created through your online activities and interactions.

Why does privacy matter?

Health information can be sensitive. Responsible digital systems should protect confidentiality, security and people's rights.

THINK: Before sharing health information, ask: Who will see it? Why is it being collected? How might it be used?

CONNECT

Telehealth & Virtual Care

Telehealth can connect people with health professionals without requiring every interaction to happen in person.

It can improve access in some circumstances, including for people who live far from services.

But ask:

  • Is virtual care appropriate for this need?
  • Is the information secure?
  • Can everyone access the technology?
  • What happens if digital care is not enough?

FUTURE OF HEALTH

Artificial Intelligence & Health

AI may transform health research, clinical care, public health and health-system management. But powerful technology must still be trustworthy.

POTENTIAL

Pattern recognition, research, decision support, drug development, health communication and public-health analysis.

QUESTIONS

Is the data representative? Is the system validated? Could it reproduce bias?

RESPONSIBILITY

AI for health should be safe, ethical, equitable, evidence-informed and accountable.

GLOBAL HEALTH & EVIDENCE

What does the evidence tell us?

World Health Organization

WHO provides global strategy and guidance on digital health, digital health systems, AI, ethics, governance and equity.

WHO — Digital Health Strategy

Digital health should be people-centred, evidence-based, safe, inclusive, equitable and appropriate to context.

WHO — AI for Health

AI should advance health while protecting safety, human rights, equity and public trust.

WHO — Ethics & Governance

Privacy, accountability, transparency, inclusiveness and human oversight matter when AI is used for health.

TEST YOUR THINKING

Digital Health Challenge

Choose an answer, then open the explanation.

1. What is digital health?

Digital health is the use of digital technologies and data to support health, healthcare and public health.

2. Why is digital health more than screen time?

Because it includes health information, health services, data, digital systems, telehealth, public health and AI.

3. What should you ask before trusting online health information?

Consider the source, evidence, date, purpose and whether trustworthy sources support the claim.

4. Why does health-data privacy matter?

Health information can be sensitive and should be protected from inappropriate access or use.

5. Can a health app replace professional judgement?

Not automatically. Digital measurements and recommendations need appropriate interpretation and context.

6. What is a digital determinant of health?

A factor such as internet access, affordability, digital literacy or accessibility that can influence people's ability to benefit from digital health.

7. Why can digital health increase inequality?

People do not have equal access to devices, internet connectivity, digital skills, accessible technologies or trustworthy information.

8. Why should AI systems be evaluated for bias?

If training data or system design do not adequately represent populations, AI can produce unfair or inaccurate results.

9. What is telehealth?

Telehealth uses digital communication technologies to support health services at a distance.

10. What is misinformation?

False or inaccurate information that may be shared without an intention to deceive.

11. Why does digital health require governance?

Because digital technologies can affect privacy, safety, equity, human rights, accountability and public trust.

12. What is the GHE principle for AI?

Use AI to help you think — not to stop you thinking.
