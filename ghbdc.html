THINK LIKE AN INVESTIGATOR

A number is only the beginning.

Good data science is not simply about making charts. It is about asking better questions, understanding where information comes from and thinking critically about what the evidence actually shows.

GHBDC encourages young investigators to look for patterns — and then ask why those patterns exist.

Begin exploring data →
QUESTION 01

What do the data show?

Describe the pattern before trying to explain it.

QUESTION 02

Why might this pattern exist?

Consider social, economic, environmental and health-system factors.

QUESTION 03

What else do we need to know?

Identify uncertainty, missing information and questions for further investigation.

A PARTNERSHIP WITH STEM FELLOWSHIP

Continuing a vision for youth, data and discovery.

In 2015, the late Mohammad Asadi Lari and Dr. Alexandre Sacha Noukhovitch co-founded STEM Fellowship, a country-wide student network created to empower young people with essential skills in data science and scholarly communication.

Through programs including the STEM Fellowship Journal, National Big Data Challenges and STEMpowerment, they inspired inquiry-driven learning among a generation of data natives while helping remove socio-economic and geographic barriers to participation in scientific disciplines.

The partnership between Global Health Explorers and STEM Fellowship through GHBDC carries this mission forward — helping inspire the next generation of global health innovators, researchers and leaders.

M
Meritorious Service Medal

Mohammad Asadi Lari was posthumously awarded the Meritorious Service Medal (Civil Division) by the Governor General of Canada, jointly recognized with Dr. Alexandre Sacha Noukhovitch for their work through STEM Fellowship.
