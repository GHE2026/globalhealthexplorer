WHY THIS MATTERS

Healthy living is more than individual choice.

Our health is influenced by what we eat, how active we are, how we sleep, our mental wellbeing, the environments around us, and the opportunities available to us.

These influences can differ between people, communities, and countries. Understanding them helps us become better Global Health Explorers.
