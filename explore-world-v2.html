Why do differences in health exist across communities and countries?

Explore how geography, society, the economy, culture, politics, and the environment influence differences in health across communities and countries—and discover how understanding these differences can inspire evidence-informed, compassionate, and innovative action.

Start Your Journey

Global Health Explorers (GHE) is a repeatable cognitive pathway that helps young people recognize differences in health, understand their underlying causes, and respond with evidence-informed, compassionate, and innovative action.

Your Learning Journey

Every Global Health Explorers module follows the same learning pathway. Rather than memorizing facts, you will gradually learn how to understand health challenges, interpret evidence, develop ideas, and contribute to healthier communities.

🌱 Learn

Build a strong foundation in global health concepts, life-course health, and the factors that influence health around the world.

🔍 Understand

Interpret reliable evidence from trusted organizations and recognize differences in health between people, communities, and countries.

⚖ Recognize

Explore how social, economic, environmental, cultural, and political factors contribute to differences in health.

💡 Imagine

Develop creative, evidence-informed ideas that could improve health and wellbeing.

🤝 Act

Transform your ideas into Global Health Mini Proposals that encourage positive change.

🌍 Lead

Share ideas, collaborate with others, and become part of a new generation of global health leaders.

🌍 Global Health Lens

Looking at health through a global health lens means understanding that health is influenced by much more than medicine. People's opportunities to live healthy lives are shaped by where they are born, grow, learn, work, live, and age.

🌎 Geography

Where people live influences climate, resources, services, and opportunities.

👨‍👩‍👧 Society

Families, education, communities, and social support shape health throughout life.

💰 Economy

Income, employment, and poverty influence nutrition, housing, and healthcare.

🏛 Politics

Public policies and leadership affect access to healthcare and public services.

🌿 Environment

Clean air, safe water, healthy food, and resilient ecosystems support healthier lives.

🎭 Culture

Beliefs, traditions, and lifestyles influence behaviours and health decisions.
