THE GHE WAY

Every difference matters. Explore it.

Countries are different. Communities within countries are different. People experience health and life in different ways.

We do not need to rush to explain every difference. First, we can notice it, explore it, and ask better questions.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Why Explore the World?

A global health explorer learns to look beyond averages and discover the patterns hidden inside them.

🌍

Countries are different

Health, education, income, environment and many other conditions vary from place to place.

🏘️

Communities are different

A national average can hide important differences between communities and population groups.

📊

Data reveals differences

Indicators help us measure and compare aspects of health and wellbeing.

🧭

Context matters

A number becomes meaningful when we understand what was measured, where, when, and for whom.

YOUR DATA EXPLORER JOURNEY

Six Steps to Discovery

You do not need to be a statistician to explore global health data. Start with curiosity.

01

FIND

Where is the information?

Find the dataset, chart, map or report — and identify who produced it.

02

UNDERSTAND

What exactly is being measured?

Look at the indicator definition, unit, population and measurement method.

03

COMPARE

Are we comparing like with like?

Check the year, population, definition and denominator before comparing places.

04

QUESTION

What might explain the difference?

Think about geography, society, environment, services and many other possible influences.

05

CONNECT

How does this connect to people's lives?

Connect data with health, social determinants, communities and the Sustainable Development Goals.

06

DISCOVER

What new question does the data create?

The best exploration may end not with an answer, but with a better question.

TRUSTED DATA GATEWAYS

Where Can We Explore?

Good data exploration begins with knowing where the data come from. These resources serve different purposes.

🏛️ OFFICIAL PUBLIC HEALTH

World Health Organization

The WHO Global Health Observatory provides health statistics across Member States, including indicators covering mortality, disease, health systems, environment and many other topics.

Explore WHO GHO ↗
🔎 DISAGGREGATED DATA

WHO Health Inequality Monitor

Explore disaggregated data showing how health and other aspects of life can differ across population groups and geographic settings.

Explore Health Inequality Monitor ↗
👧 CHILDREN & ADOLESCENTS

UNICEF Data

A major source for exploring data on children and adolescents, including health, nutrition, education, WASH, protection and other dimensions of children's lives.

Explore UNICEF Data ↗
💰 DEVELOPMENT DATA

World Bank Data

Explore internationally comparable indicators spanning health, education, poverty, environment, gender and economic development.

Explore World Bank Data ↗
🧬 ADVANCED EXPLORATION

IHME / Global Burden of Disease

An advanced gateway for exploring estimates of disease burden, causes of death, disability and related global health measures.

Our World in Data

A useful learning and visualization resource that brings together data from many providers and makes global patterns easier to explore.

DATA SOURCE LITERACY

Not Every Data Resource Has the Same Role

🏛️

Official public-health information

For current public-health decisions, check the appropriate official source — including WHO and the relevant national government or public-health authority.

Educational & exploration resources

Visualization and educational platforms can help us understand patterns and discover questions. Always look for the underlying source and definition of the data.
