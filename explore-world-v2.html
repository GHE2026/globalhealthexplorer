🌍 Social Determinants of Health (SDH)

Health is shaped by the conditions in which people are born, grow, live, work, and age. These conditions are called the Social Determinants of Health.

Structural Determinants

  • Income and social protection
  • Education
  • Social inequality
  • Governance and policy

Intermediary Determinants

  • Living conditions
  • Food security
  • Access to healthcare
  • Environmental exposure

👶 Early Childhood Development (ECD)

Early Childhood Development (ECD) refers to physical, cognitive, linguistic, and socio-emotional development from conception to 8 years of age.

0–28 days (Neonatal)

  • Survival and adaptation
  • Maternal care dependency

<1 year (Infancy)

  • Rapid physical growth
  • Immune system development

<5 years (Early childhood)

  • Brain development
  • Language acquisition
  • Social-emotional skills
