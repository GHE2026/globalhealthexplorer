OUR CORE MESSAGE

Mental health is part of health.

Everyone has mental health, and mental wellbeing can change throughout life. Asking for support is a strength—not a weakness.

Mental health is more than the absence of mental illness. It includes our emotional, psychological and social wellbeing and is influenced by individual, family, school, community and wider social conditions.

01

What Is Mental Health?

Mental health is part of everyone's health. It helps us cope with life's stresses, learn, develop our abilities, build relationships and participate in our communities.

Mental health ≠ mental illness

Everyone has mental health. Some people also experience mental-health conditions that require professional assessment, treatment, rehabilitation or ongoing support.

Mental health exists on a continuum. People can experience positive wellbeing, ordinary difficulties, significant distress or mental-health conditions at different times in their lives.

03

Stress, Pressure & Coping

Stress is part of life. Young people may experience pressure from school, examinations, family expectations, friendships, social media, uncertainty and major life changes.

Talk with someone trustworthy Maintain supportive relationships Sleep adequately Stay physically active Spend time outdoors Problem-solve Take appropriate breaks Seek support when needed
There is no single perfect coping strategy for everyone.
04

Relationships, Belonging & Connection

Supportive relationships can provide belonging, connection, encouragement and resilience.

Listen

Listen without immediately judging.

Respect

Respect differences and boundaries.

Include

Help others feel that they belong.

Support

Encourage appropriate help when needed.

Think: What would make your school a place where people feel safe asking for support?
05

Digital Life & Mental Wellbeing

Digital technology is part of everyday life. Instead of asking whether technology is simply “good” or “bad”, GHE encourages young people to examine content, context and patterns of use.

What am I seeing?
How does it make me feel?
Is it affecting my sleep?
Is it helping my relationships?
Am I comparing myself with unrealistic images?
Can I step away when I need to?

Stigma, Discrimination & Inclusion

A mental-health condition does not define a person's identity, abilities, worth or future.

07

When More Support Is Needed

GHE does not teach young people to diagnose themselves or their friends. If difficulties become persistent, overwhelming or significantly interfere with everyday life, talking with a trusted adult or qualified professional can be an important step.
