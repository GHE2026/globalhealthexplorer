GHE Resources

A growing collection of learning tools, educational materials, evidence, and opportunities designed to help young people, educators, families, and communities explore global health.

A LIVING LEARNING LIBRARY

Knowledge Should Be Accessible

Global Health Explorers is building a growing collection of resources that can help young people understand health, equity, science, data, communities, and the world around them.

Resources will be developed and curated for different audiences, recognizing that young learners, educators, parents, and community partners have different needs.

THE GHE RESOURCE LIBRARY

A Living Collection

The GHE Resource Library will grow continuously. New educational materials, evidence resources, activities, projects, and opportunities will be added as they are developed, reviewed, and curated.

Our goal is not simply to collect information. It is to help young people develop the ability to find trustworthy knowledge, ask thoughtful questions, interpret evidence, and use what they learn responsibly.

Learn Discover reliable knowledge.
Question Examine evidence critically.
Explore Connect ideas with the real world.
Act Turn learning into meaningful contribution.

OUR COMMITMENT

Knowledge With Purpose

GHE believes that access to knowledge is only the beginning. Young people need the skills to evaluate information, understand evidence, recognize uncertainty, appreciate different perspectives, and translate learning into responsible action.

We are building a resource ecosystem that supports that journey—from curiosity to knowledge, from knowledge to action, and from action to leadership.

Learn Global Health.
Think Critically.
