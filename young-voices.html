YOUNG PEOPLE • IDEAS • PERSPECTIVES

Young Voices

A space where young people can ask questions, share perspectives, express ideas, and participate in conversations about the health of our world.

YOUR VOICE MATTERS

Young People Are Part of the Conversation

Global health affects everyone—including young people. Their experiences, questions, ideas, and perspectives can enrich how communities understand and respond to health challenges.

GHE aims to create a respectful environment where young people can express themselves, learn from one another, and contribute to conversations about the world they will inherit.

A SPACE FOR PARTICIPATION

Listen. Question. Contribute.

Young Voices is built around three simple principles.

01

Listen

Listen to different experiences, perspectives, cultures, and ideas with curiosity and respect.

02

Question

Ask thoughtful questions and examine assumptions, evidence, and the information we encounter.

03

Contribute

Share ideas, stories, creative work, and perspectives that can contribute to learning and positive change.
