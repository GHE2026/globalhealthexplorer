YOUNG PEOPLE • IDEAS • PERSPECTIVES

Young Voices

A space where young people can ask questions, share perspectives, express ideas, and participate in conversations about the health of our world.

YOUR VOICE MATTERS

Young People Are Part of the Conversation

Global health affects everyone—including young people. Their experiences, questions, ideas, and perspectives can enrich how communities understand and respond to health challenges.

GHE aims to create a respectful environment where young people can express themselves, learn from one another, and contribute to conversations about the world they will inherit.

A SPACE FOR PARTICIPATION

Listen. Question. Contribute.

Young Voices is built around three simple principles.

01

Listen

Listen to different experiences, perspectives, cultures, and ideas with curiosity and respect.

02

Question

Ask thoughtful questions and examine assumptions, evidence, and the information we encounter.

03

Contribute

Share ideas, stories, creative work, and perspectives that can contribute to learning and positive change.

SHARE • CREATE • EXPLORE

What Can Young Voices Contribute?

There are many ways for young people to participate. A contribution can be a question, an idea, a story, a creative work, an observation, or evidence gathered through a project.

?

Questions

Ask the questions that others may not have considered. Curiosity is often the beginning of discovery.

✦

Stories & Experiences

Share experiences and observations that can help others understand how health and wellbeing are experienced in everyday life.

💡

Ideas & Solutions

Imagine new approaches to health challenges and propose practical ideas that could make a difference.

📊

Data & Evidence

Explore information, identify patterns, examine evidence, and communicate what the data may—and may not—tell us.

🎨

Creative Work

Use art, writing, photography, video, design, or other creative forms to communicate ideas about health and society.

🔬

Projects & Research

Turn questions into investigations, school projects, community activities, or research ideas.

FROM VOICE TO ACTION

A Voice Can Become a Journey

A question can become an investigation. An observation can become a project. A project can become a contribution. And a contribution can inspire others.

GHE encourages young people to move through this journey at their own pace, supported by learning opportunities, evidence, collaboration, and responsible guidance.

01 Question What do I want to understand?
02 Explore What evidence and perspectives can I find?
03 Create What can I develop or communicate?
04 Share How can I contribute what I have learned?
05 Inspire How might my contribution help others?

A SAFE & RESPECTFUL SPACE

Every Voice Deserves Respect

GHE aims to make Young Voices a constructive, inclusive, and respectful space for learning and participation.

01

Respect

Treat different experiences, cultures, identities, and perspectives with dignity.

02

Evidence

Distinguish evidence from opinion and be willing to examine information critically.

03

Responsibility

Share information thoughtfully and consider the potential impact of what we communicate.

04

Inclusion

Make room for voices that may otherwise be overlooked or unheard.
