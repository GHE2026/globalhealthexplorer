YOUNG VOICES • GHE

Your Voice. Your Future.

Global health is not something young people should merely study. It is something they can question, investigate, contribute to — and help shape.

Young Voices is GHE's pathway from curiosity to participation, from investigation to responsible action, and from contribution to leadership. You do not have to wait to become an expert to start making a difference.

WHY YOUNG VOICES MATTER

The challenges ahead require young minds.

BE A RESPONSIBLE INVESTIGATOR

Curiosity is powerful. Evidence makes it responsible.

GHE encourages young people to investigate real questions — while respecting people, evidence, privacy, uncertainty and the limits of their own knowledge.

You do not need to be a professional researcher. You do need to ask good questions, use credible evidence and be honest about what you know and what you do not know.

FROM QUESTION TO IDEA

Your question can become a mini-proposal.

GHE's Mini-Proposal Builder helps young people move from curiosity toward a focused, feasible and evidence-informed idea.

01
Start with something you genuinely care about.

A good proposal begins with a meaningful question, not with the desire to sound impressive.

01

Identify the Question

What health issue are you curious about?

Make it specific enough to investigate.
02

Understand the Problem

What does existing evidence tell us?

Look beyond assumptions and first impressions.
03

Identify Who Is Affected

Whose health, opportunity or experience is involved?

Consider equity, lived experience and social determinants.
04

Develop an Idea

What could reasonably be explored or improved?

Keep the idea realistic, ethical and purposeful.
05

Consider Evidence & Impact

What evidence would support your approach?

Think about feasibility, outcomes and unintended effects.

MEANINGFUL PARTICIPATION

Your voice should matter — not merely be heard.

GHE supports participation that is informed, respectful, inclusive, safe and connected to meaningful opportunities for contribution.

01

Be Informed

Learn enough about an issue to contribute thoughtfully and recognize the limits of your knowledge.

02

Be Respectful

Listen to different experiences and perspectives, especially when they challenge your assumptions.

03

Be Inclusive

Consider whose voices are present, whose may be missing and what barriers may affect participation.

04

Be Responsible

Protect dignity, privacy and safety, and think about the possible consequences of your actions.
