ABOUT THIS HUB

Personal health knowledge is a foundation for informed and responsible action.

Personal Care & Preventive Health brings together practical knowledge and skills that can help young people understand their bodies, protect themselves and others, recognize health concerns, seek appropriate help and navigate health information and services.

Health is also shaped by the conditions around us. Clean water, safe housing, education, food, transportation, income, accessible services, reliable information and supportive communities can all influence the opportunities people have to stay healthy.

The GHE approach therefore connects personal knowledge with family, peers, communities, health systems and the wider world.

Think like a Global Health Explorer

SOCIAL DETERMINANTS OF HEALTH

Prevention is personal — but health is never only personal.

The opportunity to stay healthy is influenced by conditions around us: income, housing, education, food and water, transportation, safety, employment, disability access, healthcare availability, language and social support.

A Global Health Explorer therefore looks beyond individual behaviour and asks whether systems and environments make prevention possible, accessible and fair.
