ADVOCATE FOR GHE

Sometimes participation means helping someone else discover GHE.

If you find something useful on GHE, you can share it with people who might benefit from it. This could be a classmate, teacher, school, youth group or community organization.

Advocacy is not about promoting GHE simply because it is GHE. It is about connecting people with opportunities that are genuinely useful to them.

Responsible advocacy Share what is useful. Be honest about what GHE offers. Respect people's choices.

PARTICIPATE RESPONSIBLY

Your voice matters. So does how you use it.

GHE encourages curiosity, creativity and participation, while recognizing that responsible participation means respecting others, using evidence honestly, protecting privacy and seeking appropriate guidance when needed.

Learn before you claim Listen before you judge Include before you decide Check evidence before you share Ask for help when you need it
