TOGETHER WE LEARN • TOGETHER WE LEAD

Global Health Is a Collective Endeavour

No single organization can address the complexity of global health alone. Global Health Explorers seeks to build relationships across education, public health, science, technology, community development, and youth leadership.

Through collaboration, GHE aims to connect young people with knowledge, expertise, opportunities, mentorship, and real-world challenges that can strengthen their capacity to contribute to healthier and more equitable communities.

OUR APPROACH

Partnerships for a Healthier Future

GHE seeks partnerships that expand opportunities for young people while strengthening education, evidence, equity, innovation, compassion, community, and leadership.

We value relationships built on shared purpose, mutual respect, transparency, responsible stewardship, and a genuine commitment to positive social impact.

FOUNDATIONAL & INSTITUTIONAL RELATIONSHIPS

Building the GHE Ecosystem

GHE is being developed through relationships with individuals and institutions that share an interest in education, youth development, data literacy, global health, and leadership.

FOUNDATIONAL SUPPORT

Health Education Foundation of Canada

The Health Education Foundation of Canada (HEFC) provides an important institutional context for advancing health education, public engagement, and the development of future-oriented initiatives.

GHE seeks to build on this foundation by creating meaningful educational opportunities for young people interested in health and global citizenship.

EDUCATION & INNOVATION

STEM Fellowship

STEM Fellowship brings expertise in science, technology, data, research, and youth education. Its experience in student-focused data initiatives provides an important foundation for the development of the Global Health Big Data Challenge.

GHE aims to strengthen this connection by bringing global health questions into youth-oriented STEM learning and data exploration.

ACADEMIC COMMUNITY

Universities & Researchers

GHE seeks to connect with universities, researchers, educators, and students who can contribute scientific expertise, mentorship, educational resources, and opportunities for young learners.

These relationships can help bridge education, research, and real-world global health challenges.

PARTNERSHIP PRINCIPLES

What Makes a GHE Partnership Meaningful?

GHE seeks relationships that are grounded in shared purpose and that create meaningful value for young people, educators, communities, and the broader global health ecosystem.

01

Shared Purpose

Partners share a genuine commitment to education, health, equity, youth development, or positive social impact.

02

Mutual Value

Collaboration should create meaningful value for both partners while keeping the interests of learners and communities at the centre.

03

Integrity

Partnerships should be transparent, ethical, responsible, and consistent with the Constitution and Five Pillars of GHE.

04

Long-Term Impact

GHE values relationships that can contribute to sustainable learning, leadership, innovation, and healthier communities over time.
