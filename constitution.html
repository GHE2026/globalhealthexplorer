The Constitution of
Global Health Explorers (GHE)

Building the Next Generation of Global Health Leaders

Version 1.0 — August 2026

Preamble

The twenty-first century presents humanity with unprecedented opportunities and unprecedented challenges. Climate change, pandemics, technological transformation, widening inequities, humanitarian crises, demographic change, and rapidly evolving health systems require a generation capable of thinking critically, acting compassionately, and collaborating across cultures and disciplines.

Global Health Explorers (GHE) is a global health education platform dedicated to developing the next generation of global health leaders. GHE believes that global health education should begin early, continue throughout life, and empower children and young people to become informed citizens, compassionate leaders, responsible innovators, and active contributors to the health and well-being of their communities and the wider world.

Through evidence-informed learning, interdisciplinary thinking, responsible innovation, meaningful community engagement, continuous learning, and a commitment to sustainability and continuous improvement, GHE seeks to inspire a generation that understands health not merely as the absence of disease, but as a shared human responsibility rooted in equity, dignity, sustainability, resilience, and global solidarity.

This Constitution establishes the enduring principles, values, governance, and educational commitments that guide Global Health Explorers and all its present and future initiatives.

Article I — Identity

Global Health Explorers (GHE) is an independent global health education platform committed to empowering children and young people through high-quality, equitable, evidence-informed, and innovative learning.

GHE exists to foster knowledge, values, leadership, and action that contribute to healthier communities and a healthier world.

Article II — Vision

Our vision is a world where every child and young person is empowered to understand, improve, responsibly collaborate, and lead global health through knowledge, compassion, innovation, and action.

Article III — Mission

Our mission is to develop the next generation of global health leaders through innovative, equitable, and evidence-informed learning that empowers children and young people to improve the health of their communities and the world.

Article IV — Core Values

GHE is guided by the enduring values of curiosity, equity, integrity, compassion, scientific excellence, innovation, and global citizenship.

These values shape every educational programme, partnership, decision, and interaction across the organization.

Article V — Guiding Principles

GHE is founded upon eight guiding principles:

  • Learner-centred education
  • Evidence-informed practice
  • Equity and inclusion
  • Innovation with responsibility
  • Collaboration and global citizenship
  • Continuous learning
  • Continuous improvement
  • Sustainability

Together, these principles provide the ethical and educational foundation for all present and future activities of GHE.

Article VI — The Five Pillars

The work of GHE is organized around five interconnected pillars:

1. Education

Developing accessible, engaging, and evidence-informed learning opportunities for children and young people.

2. Data and Innovation

Promoting data literacy, responsible technology, and innovative solutions for global health challenges.

3. Leadership and Action

Supporting young people to transform knowledge into meaningful community action.

4. Research and Partnerships

Building collaboration among learners, educators, researchers, institutions, and communities.

5. Impact and Sustainability

Creating lasting value through measurable impact, responsible stewardship, and long-term growth.

These pillars guide the design, delivery, evaluation, and future growth of the organization.

Article VII — Educational Commitment

GHE is committed to providing accessible, engaging, inclusive, and evidence-informed educational opportunities that encourage curiosity, critical thinking, creativity, ethical leadership, and lifelong learning.

Education shall connect knowledge with action, enabling learners to contribute meaningfully to their families, communities, and the wider world.

Article VIII — Governance

GHE shall be governed according to the principles of transparency, accountability, scientific integrity, ethical leadership, child safeguarding, and responsible stewardship.

Governance shall support academic excellence, public trust, and continuous organizational learning.

Article IX — Partnerships

GHE recognizes that meaningful change is achieved through collaboration.

We are committed to working with learners, families, educators, schools, universities, governments, Indigenous communities, civil society, professional organizations, foundations, and international partners to strengthen global health education and expand opportunities for young people worldwide.

Article X — Continuous Improvement

GHE is a learning organization.

We continuously evaluate, reflect, innovate, and improve our educational programmes, technologies, partnerships, and organizational practices through evidence, research, stakeholder engagement, and measurable outcomes.

While our methods evolve, our vision, mission, and values remain constant.

Article XI — Sustainability

GHE is committed to building a sustainable educational movement that creates lasting value for learners and society.

Sustainability shall guide educational practice, organizational development, financial stewardship, environmental responsibility, technological resilience, and long-term institutional growth so that future generations may continue to benefit from our work.

Article XII — Amendments and Final Provisions

This Constitution provides the enduring foundation of Global Health Explorers.

It may evolve through thoughtful and transparent governance as GHE grows and learns.

Any amendment shall preserve the organization's vision, mission, core values, and guiding principles while strengthening its ability to serve future generations.

Constitutional Commitment

The future of global health depends not only on stronger health systems, but also on empowered children and young people.

By investing in the next generation and guided by the principles of this Constitution, Global Health Explorers (GHE) affirms its enduring commitment to inspiring and equipping present and future generations to build a healthier, more equitable, and more sustainable world.

Inspired by remarkable lives.
Dedicated to future generations.
Committed to a healthier world.
