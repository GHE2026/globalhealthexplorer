WHY INNOVATION MATTERS

Global health challenges need more than technology.

Around the world, people face health challenges that cannot be solved by one discipline, one technology or one person.

They need people who can understand problems, question assumptions, examine evidence, imagine possibilities and work with others to create better solutions.

Innovation can happen in a laboratory, a hospital, a school, a community, a health system or through a mobile phone.

But innovation is not simply about making something new. Meaningful innovation addresses a real need, is supported by evidence, works in context and improves people's lives without creating unacceptable harms or widening inequities.

FEATURED FRONTIER

Genomics & Precision Medicine

What if health care could better account for differences between people?

Genomics helps scientists understand the information contained in DNA. Precision medicine combines genomic, molecular, clinical and other health information to support more targeted approaches to prevention, diagnosis and treatment.

But precision must also mean precision about equity . If genomic data do not represent diverse populations, innovations may work better for some groups than others.

THINK BEFORE YOU BUILD

Responsible Innovation

New does not automatically mean better. Responsible innovators ask not only “Can we?”, but also “Should we?” and “For whom?”

01

Evidence

Is there credible evidence that the idea works?

02

Safety

What could go wrong, and how can risks be reduced?

03

Equity

Who benefits? Who might be left behind?

04

Ethics

Does the innovation respect rights, dignity, autonomy and fairness?

05

Privacy & Security

Are personal information and digital systems protected appropriately?

06

Context

Does it fit the people, culture, resources and health system where it will be used?

07

Sustainability

Can the solution continue to create value without creating unacceptable costs?

08

Accountability

Who is responsible for decisions, outcomes and unintended consequences?

!
A responsible innovation question:

Before moving from prototype to implementation, ask what evidence, safeguards, participation, governance and monitoring are needed.
