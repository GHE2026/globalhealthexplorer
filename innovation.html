WHY IT MATTERS

Global health challenges need more than technology.

They need people who can understand problems, question assumptions, examine evidence, imagine possibilities and work with others to create better solutions.

Innovation can happen in a laboratory, a hospital, a school, a community, a health system or through a mobile phone.

💡

Evidence + empathy + creativity

Meaningful innovation connects scientific evidence with human needs and creative problem-solving.

THINK BEFORE YOU BUILD

Responsible Innovation

A solution is not truly innovative simply because it is new. We must ask whether it is safe, effective, equitable, ethical, accessible and sustainable.

01

Evidence

Does the solution work?

02

Safety

Could it cause harm?

03

Equity

Who benefits and who might be left behind?

04

Ethics

Is it fair and respectful?

05

Accessibility

Can everyone who needs it use it?

06

Sustainability

Can it create lasting positive impact?
