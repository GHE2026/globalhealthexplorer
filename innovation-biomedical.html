THE BIG IDEA

What if understanding life could help us redesign health?

Biomedical innovation starts with biology. Scientists study cells, genes, proteins, immune systems and the human body. Engineers and health professionals then use that knowledge to create technologies, treatments and tools.

THINK DEEPLY

Biomedical innovation must serve people.

A scientific breakthrough is only part of the journey. We also need to ask whether a new treatment or technology is safe, effective, affordable, accessible and fair.

Ask yourself:

  • Does the evidence support it?
  • Is it safe?
  • Who can access it?
  • Who might be left behind?
  • What are the ethical questions?
  • Will it create meaningful health impact?
