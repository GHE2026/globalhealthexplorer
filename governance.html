TRUST • ACCOUNTABILITY • STEWARDSHIP

Governance

Building an organization that is responsible, transparent, inclusive, and worthy of the trust placed in it.

GOVERNING WITH PURPOSE

Responsible Stewardship

Global Health Explorers is committed to building governance structures that protect its mission, support its learners, strengthen accountability, and ensure that decisions are made in the best interests of the communities it seeks to serve.

Governance at GHE is understood not simply as organizational administration, but as stewardship of a long-term educational and public-interest mission.

OUR GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

How We Aim to Govern

As GHE develops, its governance arrangements will be guided by a set of core principles designed to protect trust, encourage participation, and support responsible growth.

01

Accountability

GHE seeks to maintain clear responsibilities, appropriate oversight, and transparent decision- making across its activities and partnerships.

02

Transparency

Important decisions, institutional commitments, partnerships, and the use of resources should be communicated clearly and responsibly.

03

Inclusion

GHE seeks to create meaningful opportunities for diverse voices, perspectives, and forms of expertise to contribute to its development.

04

Integrity & Ethics

GHE seeks to uphold high standards of integrity, ethical conduct, responsible research, and respect for the dignity and rights of all participants and communities.

05

Youth Voice

Young people are not simply recipients of education. GHE seeks to create meaningful opportunities for youth perspectives to inform learning, programs, innovation, and future directions.

06

Safeguarding

The safety, dignity, privacy, and wellbeing of children and young people are fundamental considerations in the design and delivery of GHE activities.

PARTNERSHIP STEWARDSHIP

Building Trust Through Collaboration

GHE recognizes that meaningful global health education requires collaboration across schools, universities, public health institutions, community organizations, technology partners, educators, families, and young people.

Partnerships will therefore be developed with attention to shared values, clarity of roles, mutual respect, appropriate recognition, and responsible stewardship of resources and information.

GHE will seek partnerships that strengthen its educational mission while protecting the interests and wellbeing of its learners and communities.

INDEPENDENCE & TRUST

Managing Conflicts of Interest

GHE recognizes that individuals involved in governance, advising, education, research, partnerships, or other activities may sometimes have interests that could influence—or appear to influence—their responsibilities.

Our approach

  • Encourage disclosure of relevant interests.
  • Separate personal interests from institutional decision-making where appropriate.
  • Maintain transparency around significant institutional relationships.
  • Protect the credibility and independence of GHE's educational mission.

RESPONSIBLE DATA STEWARDSHIP

Protecting Information and Privacy

As GHE increasingly works with digital learning, data literacy, artificial intelligence, and potentially sensitive information, responsible data stewardship will remain an essential part of its governance approach.

GHE will seek to promote privacy, security, informed participation, appropriate data use, transparency, and ethical consideration of emerging technologies.

These principles are particularly important when activities involve children and young people.

CONTINUOUS ACCOUNTABILITY

Governance at GHE is intended to evolve through reflection, learning, consultation, and continuous improvement. The organization will seek to learn from experience and respond responsibly as its activities and partnerships develop.

INSTITUTIONAL FOUNDATION

Governance Rooted in the Constitution

The Constitution establishes the fundamental purpose, values, and commitments of Global Health Explorers. Governance provides the structures and practices through which those commitments can be protected and implemented.

The Five Pillars then translate these principles into a framework for learning, equity, evidence, compassion, community, leadership, and action.

LOOKING AHEAD

Governance That Grows Responsibly

As Global Health Explorers grows, its governance arrangements may evolve to include appropriate boards, advisory structures, youth representation, educational and scientific expertise, safeguarding mechanisms, and other forms of independent guidance.

Any future structure should remain proportionate to the organization's activities and consistent with its public-interest mission, legal obligations, and commitment to transparency and accountability.

The objective is not governance for its own sake, but governance that enables GHE to remain trustworthy, effective, inclusive, and focused on its mission.

OUR COMMITMENT

Trust Is a Responsibility

Global Health Explorers seeks to earn trust through responsible leadership, transparent practices, meaningful participation, ethical conduct, and continuous learning.

We will strive to build an organization in which young people can learn, contribute, and lead with confidence—and in which every partnership and institutional decision strengthens the possibility of a healthier and more equitable world.
