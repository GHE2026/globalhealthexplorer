Who We Are

Global Health Explorers (GHE)

Learning Global Health Today, Leading Tomorrow

Our Identity

Global Health Explorers (GHE) is a global health education platform dedicated to developing the next generation of global health leaders through innovative, equitable, and evidence-informed learning.

GHE empowers children and young people with knowledge, values, analytical skills, and leadership capacity needed to understand and address global health challenges in their communities and beyond.

Our Story

Global Health Explorers was created from a vision that young people can become powerful contributors to a healthier, more equitable, and sustainable world.

Founded by Zoha Asadi-Lari, GHE reflects a commitment to empower children and adolescents with global health knowledge, curiosity, compassion, and leadership skills.

Inspired by the memories and contributions of Mohammad and Zeynab , GHE was created as a commitment to invest in the next generation — transforming love, knowledge, and hope into opportunities for young people everywhere to learn, lead, and create positive change.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

A world where every child and young person is empowered to understand, improve, responsibly collaborate, and lead global health through knowledge, compassion, innovation, and action.

Our Mission

To develop the next generation of global health leaders through innovative, equitable, and evidence-informed learning that empowers children and young people to improve the health of their communities and the world.

Our Educational Philosophy

Education

Education is a foundation for health resilience, equity, sustainability, and global citizenship.

Empowerment

Young people are not only future leaders; they are active contributors to healthier communities today.

Action

Knowledge becomes meaningful when translated into responsible innovation and community engagement.

Our Five Pillars

1. Education

Building accessible, engaging, and evidence-informed global health learning.

2. Data and Innovation

Developing data literacy, analytical thinking, and responsible innovation.

3. Leadership and Action

Transforming knowledge into community action and global leadership.

4. Research and Partnerships

Connecting learners with educators, scientists, and global partners.

5. Impact and Sustainability

Creating lasting educational, social, environmental, and organizational value.

Our Foundation

The GHE Constitution establishes the enduring principles, values, governance, and educational commitments that guide all present and future initiatives of Global Health Explorers.
