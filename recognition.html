WHAT COUNTS

We recognize more than outcomes.

A meaningful contribution is not always the biggest project or the most visible achievement. GHE looks at how young people learn, work with others and use their knowledge responsibly.

01

Learning

Growth in knowledge, skills, critical thinking and understanding.

02

Initiative

The willingness to move from curiosity toward constructive participation.

03

Evidence

Respect for evidence, uncertainty, critical thinking and responsible claims.

04

Collaboration

Working respectfully across different perspectives, backgrounds and experiences.

05

Responsibility

Considering consequences, fairness, safety and who may be affected by an action.

06

Service

Helping others learn, participate and have opportunities to move forward.

THE ASPIRATIONAL DESTINATION

What is a GHE World Champion?

A GHE World Champion is not simply someone who has won something.

It is an aspirational idea for a young person who has developed the knowledge, character and experience to use learning responsibly, help others participate and contribute to healthier and fairer communities.

World Champions represent the direction GHE hopes young people can grow toward: from learning, to contribution, to leadership, to inspiration.

THE GHE WORLD CHAMPION LENS

Not the best. Not the loudest. The most responsible.

The idea of a World Champion reflects a deeper definition of achievement: what you know matters, but so does what you do with it and how you affect others.

01

Learns continuously

Remains curious and recognizes that learning never really ends.

02

Thinks critically

Questions assumptions, examines evidence and is comfortable with uncertainty.

03

Includes others

Creates space for different perspectives and recognizes the value of other voices.

04

Acts responsibly

Thinks about consequences, fairness, safety and real-world impact.

05

Helps others grow

Uses leadership to create opportunities for others rather than simply gaining recognition for themselves.

06

Serves the bigger picture

Connects personal action with communities, systems and the wider world.

★

RECOGNITION WITH PURPOSE

The goal is not to create winners. It is to help create people who help others win.

Recognition should encourage young people to keep learning, take responsible action, support their peers and imagine what they can contribute to the world.
