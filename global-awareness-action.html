03 GLOBAL AWARENESS PATHWAY

From Awareness to Action

Move from knowing about global health to knowing what you can do—individually and together—when a trusted public-health authority announces a threat.

The goal is not professional epidemiology. It is responsible, age-appropriate participation: know, verify, protect, communicate, support and act.

FROM AWARENESS TO ACTION
04 · WHAT CAN I DO?

When a trusted authority announces a health threat.

GHE asks a practical adolescent question: What should I know, what can I do, and how can I participate?

SELF Know: the official message, risks and what is still uncertain. Do: follow appropriate guidance, protect yourself and avoid rumours. Participate: stay informed from trusted sources.
FAMILY & PEERS Know where reliable information comes from. Talk, listen, support and avoid stigma. Share accurate information and challenge misinformation respectfully.
SCHOOL Know the school plan and expectations. Follow appropriate guidance and participate in preparedness. Help create a safe, respectful and informed environment.
COMMUNITY Know local resources and support services. Help neighbours and people who may need support. Listen to community voices and participate responsibly.
TOWN / CITY Know local services and emergency information. Follow local guidance and use services responsibly. Support local efforts and provide feedback through appropriate channels.
PROVINCE / STATE Know where official regional information comes from. Follow regional guidance. Contribute to solutions and stay informed.
COUNTRY Know national public-health guidance and responsibilities. Follow national measures and recommendations. Participate in civic life and support equity.
WORLD Know why global cooperation matters. Support solidarity, equity and access to health tools. Be a global citizen: think global, act local.
VACCINES & ACTION

Prevention is participatory.

Vaccination is one example of how individual decisions, public-health programs and global cooperation can intersect.

LEARN Understand what vaccines do and use trusted sources for current recommendations.
CHECK Question vaccine claims using the GHE Information Check.
RESPECT Discuss questions without stigma, ridicule or pressure.
PARTICIPATE Support accurate information and community health efforts appropriate to your age and setting.
FROM AWARENESS TO ACTION

Awareness → Understanding → Participation → Responsible Action

Global citizenship is not about doing everything. It is about knowing what is appropriate for you, acting responsibly and contributing to healthier communities.

01 · AWARENESS I know that health threats can affect connected communities.
02 · UNDERSTANDING I can find and interpret reliable information.
03 · PARTICIPATION I can help my family, school and community respond respectfully.
04 · RESPONSIBLE ACTION I can act within my role and support evidence-informed cooperation.
REFLECT

Ask yourself

Could I explain why trusted sources matter? Could I recognize a misleading health claim? Could I help someone find official information without spreading fear or stigma?

GLOBAL LEADERSHIP

Lead by being trustworthy.

Good global health citizenship begins with humility, evidence, respect, listening and a willingness to correct ourselves when better information becomes available.
