Enter the World of Biological Innovation

You do not need a laboratory to start thinking like a scientist.

Biological innovation begins long before an experiment. It begins with curiosity: noticing a problem, asking a question, searching for evidence, comparing ideas, and imagining what might be possible.

GHE provides a safe gateway into this world. You will explore real scientific questions without being asked to perform laboratory procedures or develop real biological products.

💡

Think like a scientist — safely.

Your task is not to manufacture a vaccine or conduct laboratory research. Your task is to understand the science, ask meaningful questions, investigate evidence, and develop an informed idea.

Basic Vaccinology Gateway

Ten questions can open the world of vaccinology.

You are not expected to become a vaccine scientist overnight. Start with questions. Follow the evidence. Build your understanding step by step.

01

THE PROBLEM

Why Vaccines?

What problem are vaccines trying to solve? Why have vaccines changed population health?

  • Why do some diseases have vaccines while others do not?
  • What does prevention mean?
  • How can vaccination affect population health?
02

IMMUNITY

How Immunity Works

How does the immune system recognize, respond to, and remember threats?

  • What is an immune response?
  • What is immune memory?
  • Why does immune memory matter for vaccines?
03

MECHANISM

How Vaccines Work

How can vaccination prepare the immune system before exposure to a disease?

  • What does a vaccine present to the immune system?
  • Why can different vaccine platforms work?
  • What happens after vaccination?
04

DESIGN

How Vaccines Are Designed

What are scientists trying to achieve when they design a vaccine?

  • What biological target should be recognized?
  • Which vaccine platform might be appropriate?
  • How do scientists balance potential benefits and risks?
05

EVIDENCE

How Scientists Know a Vaccine Works

How can researchers determine whether a vaccine is safe and effective?

  • What is a clinical trial?
  • Why are different phases needed?
  • What does vaccine effectiveness mean?
06

MANUFACTURING

How Vaccines Are Made

How does a scientific concept move toward a manufactured vaccine?

  • Why does manufacturing matter?
  • What does quality control mean?
  • Why must manufacturing be consistent?
07

TECHNOLOGY & LOGISTICS

How Vaccines Reach People

How do we keep vaccines safe and usable from production to administration?

  • What is the cold chain?
  • Why does temperature matter?
  • How are vaccines monitored during transport?
  • What technologies could improve vaccine logistics?

Cancer Vaccines

Frontier Case Study

Cancer Vaccines: Could a vaccine be personalized?

Infectious diseases and cancer are very different, yet both raise an extraordinary scientific question: can the immune system be guided to recognize a harmful target?

Researchers are investigating personalized cancer vaccines that use information about an individual's tumour to identify targets that the immune system might recognize.

If you were becoming a cancer scientist, what sort of vaccine would you design?
