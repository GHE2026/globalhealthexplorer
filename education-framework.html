A lifelong learning framework empowering children and young people with knowledge, values, skills, and opportunities to improve health and well-being in their communities and the world.

Why Global Health Education?

The health challenges of the twenty-first century require more than scientific knowledge alone. They require curiosity, critical thinking, compassion, collaboration, and responsible leadership.

Global Health Explorers believes that preparing future generations must begin early by helping children and young people understand the connections between health, society, environment, technology, equity, and human development.

Through accessible and engaging education, GHE creates opportunities for learners to explore global health challenges and become active contributors to healthier communities.

GHE Learning Philosophy

The educational approach of GHE is based on the principle that learning should move beyond information acquisition toward reflection, action, and leadership.

Explore

Discover global health challenges and develop curiosity.

Learn

Build knowledge through evidence-informed education.

Reflect

Connect knowledge with personal values and communities.

Act

Transform learning into meaningful action.

Lead

Inspire others and contribute to a healthier world.

GHE Learning Pathways

The Global Health Explorers learning pathway consists of eight interconnected modules designed to introduce learners to essential global health concepts, develop analytical skills, and encourage responsible action.

1. Healthy Living

Understanding personal health, prevention, nutrition, physical activity, and healthy choices.

2. Explore the World

Discovering global communities, cultures, health systems, and the social determinants of health.

3. Global Awareness

Exploring pandemics, climate change, humanitarian emergencies, and global health challenges.

4. Mental Health

Promoting emotional well-being, resilience, empathy, and supportive communities.

5. Indigenous Health

Learning about Indigenous perspectives, health equity, history, culture, and reconciliation.

6. Teachers & Parents Resources

Supporting educators and families with resources to facilitate global health learning.

7. Global Health Heroes

Learning from individuals and communities who have contributed to health, science, and social progress.

8. Global Health Big Data Challenge (GHBDC)

Developing data literacy, analytical thinking, and responsible innovation through real-world global health challenges.

Global Health Big Data Challenge (GHBDC)

The GHBDC is a flagship initiative of Global Health Explorers that connects young learners with the power of data, science, and technology to understand and address global health challenges.

Through guided exploration of real-world datasets, students develop skills in data interpretation, visualization, critical thinking, collaboration, and ethical innovation.

Global Health Education and Sustainable Development

GHE aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), recognizing that health is interconnected with education, equity, climate, innovation, communities, and human development.

Through global health education, young people develop the knowledge and skills needed to contribute to a more equitable, sustainable, and healthier world.

Health • Equity • Education • Sustainability • Innovation • Partnership

Looking Toward the Future

Global Health Explorers aims to become a global learning movement where children and young people from diverse backgrounds can explore health challenges, develop leadership skills, and collaborate across communities and cultures.

By nurturing curiosity, compassion, scientific thinking, and responsible innovation, GHE is investing in the next generation of leaders who will help build a healthier world.
