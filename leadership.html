THE LEADERSHIP CAPABILITY FRAMEWORK

LEADS

The Canadian LEADS in a Caring Environment framework provides a way to understand leadership capabilities across health systems.

GHE presents the framework for educational purposes and does not claim ownership of the LEADS framework. The framework is associated with the Canadian College of Health Leaders.

L

LEAD SELF

Develop yourself.

Build character, self-awareness, curiosity, confidence and perseverance. Leadership begins with responsibility for one's own learning, decisions and behaviour.

Character Integrity Curiosity Perseverance
E

ENGAGE OTHERS

Build relationships.

Listen carefully, communicate respectfully, encourage participation and create space for different perspectives and experiences.

Listening Empathy Inclusion Respect
A

ACHIEVE RESULTS

Turn ideas into action.

Connect purpose with evidence, make thoughtful decisions, take action, assess what happens and improve when results do not match expectations.

Evidence Action Evaluation Responsibility
D

DEVELOP COALITIONS

Work across differences.

Build relationships across people, schools, communities and organizations. Bring different knowledge, perspectives and strengths together.

Collaboration Partnership Trust Knowledge
S

SHAPE SYSTEMS

Shape Systems / Systems Transformation.

See beyond individual situations. Understand how people, policies, environments, institutions, resources and power interact—and help create sustainable improvement.

Systems Thinking Equity Innovation Change
LEAD SELF → ENGAGE OTHERS → ACHIEVE RESULTS → DEVELOP COALITIONS → SHAPE SYSTEMS
Systems Transformation GHE preserves this concept because meaningful global health improvement often requires change beyond individual behaviour or individual projects.

THE GHE LENS

GHE

If LEADS describes what leaders develop, GHE asks how those capabilities are used.

G

GIVE / SERVE

Use leadership for others.

Leadership is not simply personal achievement. Use your knowledge, skills, time and opportunities to contribute to the wellbeing of others—especially people affected by health inequities.

Healthy Living Global Health Recognition
H

HEAR / HUMANIZE / HELP

Put people at the centre.

Listen to lived experience. Humanize health issues by seeing people in their social, cultural and environmental contexts. Understand the social determinants of health (SDH), respect dignity and diverse perspectives, and help people move forward.

Mental Health Research Young Voices SDH & Global Health
E

EMPOWER / EMPATHY

Help others participate and grow.

Use empathy to understand people and empowerment to help them participate, develop confidence, contribute meaningfully and eventually become leaders themselves.

Young Voices Help Build GHE Recognition
