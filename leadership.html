LEADERSHIP AT GHE

So, who is a good leader?

A GHE leader is not someone who stands above others. A GHE leader is someone who helps others move forward.

01

Leadership is a practice

You do not need a position to lead. Leadership can begin with asking a thoughtful question, listening carefully, helping a teammate or taking responsibility for a task.

02

Leadership is relational

Strong leaders make room for other people. They listen, include different perspectives, encourage others and recognize that everyone has something to contribute.

03

Leadership is responsible

Good intentions are not enough. Leaders think about evidence, consequences, fairness, safety and who may benefit or be affected by an action.

THE GHE LEADERSHIP CHARTER

LEADS-GHE

A practical leadership architecture for turning learning into responsible impact.

LEADS-GHE is GHE's youth-oriented adaptation of the LEADS leadership architecture, interpreted through GHE's values of integrity, empathy, evidence, responsibility, inclusion, perseverance and service.

L
LEAD SELF

Know yourself. Build character.

Develop integrity, curiosity, confidence, self-awareness and perseverance. A leader begins by taking responsibility for their own learning and behaviour.

Integrity Curiosity Perseverance
E
ENGAGE OTHERS

Listen. Include. Encourage.

Leadership grows through relationships. Listen to understand, value different experiences and help others discover what they can contribute.

Empathy Listening Inclusion
A
ACHIEVE RESULTS

Turn ideas into responsible action.

Move from questions to evidence, from evidence to ideas and from ideas to action. Define a meaningful goal, learn from results and improve.

Evidence Critical Thinking Responsibility
D
DEVELOP COALITIONS

Work across differences.

Meaningful change rarely happens alone. Build respectful partnerships with classmates, teachers, families, communities and organizations.

Collaboration Respect Partnership
S
TRANSFORM SYSTEMS

See the bigger picture.

Ask how people, policies, environments, institutions and resources interact. Look beyond individual problems and imagine how systems can work better.

Systems Thinking Equity Impact
LEAD SELF → ENGAGE OTHERS → ACHIEVE RESULTS → DEVELOP COALITIONS → TRANSFORM SYSTEMS

THE GHE LENS

LEADS tells us how to lead. GHE values tell us how to lead well.

The GHE approach does not treat leadership as a race for status or authority. It asks whether leadership is being practiced with integrity, humanity, evidence and responsibility.

What am I doing? Purpose & responsibility
Who am I doing it with? Listening & collaboration
Who is affected? Empathy & inclusion
Who benefits? Equity & service
Could this make the system better? Evidence & systems thinking
01

Integrity

Do the right thing, even when nobody is watching.

02

Empathy

Understand people before trying to lead them.

03

Evidence

Question assumptions and learn from trustworthy evidence.

04

Responsibility

Own your decisions and consider their consequences.

05

Inclusion

Make room for voices, experiences and perspectives that may be overlooked.

06

Perseverance

Learn, adapt, seek help and keep moving forward responsibly.

07

Service

Use what you learn to make things better for others.
