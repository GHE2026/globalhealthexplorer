FROM LEARNING TO LEADERSHIP

Take Action

Global health is not only something to study. It is something we can understand, question, influence, and improve.

YOUR JOURNEY CONTINUES

Knowledge Becomes Meaningful When We Use It

GHE encourages young people to move from curiosity to understanding, from understanding to action, and from action toward responsible leadership.

Taking action does not always mean solving a global problem. Sometimes it begins with asking a better question, sharing reliable information, helping someone in your community, protecting the environment, or using evidence to challenge an assumption.

THE ACTION PATHWAY

Five Ways to Turn Learning Into Action

Every meaningful contribution begins somewhere. GHE provides young people with multiple ways to transform knowledge into participation.

01

Ask

Ask thoughtful questions about health, equity, evidence, and the world around you.

02

Explore

Investigate information, perspectives, data, and experiences before reaching conclusions.

03

Create

Develop ideas, projects, stories, research, or solutions that respond to real challenges.

04

Contribute

Share your knowledge and skills in your school, family, community, or wider world.

05

Lead

Inspire others, build partnerships, and help move ideas toward meaningful change.

RECOGNITION & ACHIEVEMENT

Celebrate Learning. Recognize Contribution.

GHE believes that recognition should encourage learning and contribution rather than competition alone.

Young people may be recognized for curiosity, creativity, collaboration, evidence-based thinking, community contribution, innovation, and leadership.

Recognition is therefore part of the learning journey: it helps young people see that their ideas, efforts, and contributions matter.

✦ Learning Recognizing curiosity and growth.
✦ Creativity Celebrating original ideas and solutions.
✦ Contribution Valuing service and meaningful participation.
✦ Leadership Encouraging responsible influence and action.

THE GHE COMMITMENT

Learn. Act. Lead.

Global Health Explorers believes that young people can help build a healthier and more equitable future when they have access to knowledge, evidence, opportunity, and supportive communities.

We invite every Explorer to begin where they are, learn what they can, contribute what they can, and gradually discover the leader within.
