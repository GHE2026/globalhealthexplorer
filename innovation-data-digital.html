THE BIG IDEA

How does data become innovation?

Health innovation increasingly depends on the ability to collect, understand and use information responsibly. Data science, digital health and artificial intelligence are connected—but they are not the same thing.

RESPONSIBLE DIGITAL INNOVATION

AI and digital health can create enormous opportunities, but they can also create bias, privacy risks, misinformation, exclusion and unintended consequences.
