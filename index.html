WELCOME TO GHE

The world is your classroom.

Global Health Explorers is an educational platform for young people who want to understand health beyond the walls of a hospital — and discover how health connects us to society, communities, countries and the world.

01

Explore

Discover health questions that begin with you and expand outward to your family, community, country and the world.

02

Think

Ask questions, examine evidence, challenge assumptions and understand why health is not equally experienced by everyone.

03

Connect

See the connections between health, society, environment, technology, equity, the Sustainable Development Goals and global change.

04

Lead

Turn knowledge into constructive action and become part of the next generation of global health leaders.
