GLOBAL HEALTH BIG DATA CHALLENGE

What is GHBDC?

GHBDC helps young people turn curiosity about global health into questions, investigations, evidence and ideas for action.

The Global Health Big Data Challenge is an inquiry-driven learning experience where students and young investigators explore real-world health data, ask meaningful questions, discover patterns and communicate what they learn.

THE IDEA

Data can help us ask better questions.

Global health is full of important questions. Data gives us another way to investigate them.

01

Start with curiosity.

GHBDC begins with questions rather than predetermined answers. Participants are encouraged to notice something interesting, surprising or important and ask why it might be happening.

02

Investigate with evidence.

Participants learn to work with real-world datasets and evidence to investigate health patterns across populations, places and time.

03

Communicate what you discover.

A good investigation does not end with a graph. GHBDC encourages participants to interpret their findings, communicate them clearly and consider what they might mean.

WHY DATA MATTERS

Global health leaves patterns.

Health is shaped by many interconnected factors. Population data can help us see relationships that are difficult to understand from individual experiences alone.

By exploring data across countries, communities, age groups, time periods and other dimensions, young investigators can begin to understand how health challenges are distributed — and where inequalities may exist.

THE GHBDC APPROACH

More than a data challenge.

01

Inquiry-driven

Participants begin with questions and learn to investigate rather than simply reproduce answers.

02

Evidence-based

Real-world data and credible sources form the foundation of the learning process.

03

Youth-centered

Young people are not simply audiences for global health education — they are investigators and contributors.

04

Globally connected

Health questions cross borders. GHBDC encourages participants to think across populations, places and systems.

05

Accessible by design

Participants can enter through different pathways, backgrounds and levels of technical experience.

06

Connected to action

The ultimate question is not only what the data show, but what we might learn, imagine and do next.

A SHARED MISSION

Building on a legacy of youth empowerment.

GHBDC is developed in partnership with STEM Fellowship, the student-centered network co-founded in 2015 by Mohammad Asadi Lari and Dr. Alexandre Sacha Noukhovitch.

Their vision of empowering young people with data science, scholarly communication and inquiry-driven learning remains central to this work.
