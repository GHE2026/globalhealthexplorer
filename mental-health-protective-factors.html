What Helps Mental Health?

Mental health is influenced by many interconnected protective factors. Some are personal, while others depend on families, schools, communities and wider society.

01

Supportive Relationships

Caring relationships with family members, friends, teachers, mentors and other trusted people can provide belonging, encouragement and practical support.

02

Safe & Inclusive Environments

Feeling physically and emotionally safe at home, at school and in the community can support healthy development and wellbeing.

03

Healthy Daily Habits

Regular physical activity, adequate sleep, nutritious food, time outdoors and meaningful activities can contribute to physical and mental wellbeing.

04

Skills for Coping

Learning how to recognize emotions, solve problems, manage stress and communicate effectively can strengthen our ability to deal with challenges.

05

Meaning & Purpose

Learning, creativity, helping others, cultural connection, hobbies and meaningful goals can give people a sense of purpose and belonging.

06

Access to Support

Accessible, respectful and appropriate health and social services can help people receive support when challenges become difficult to manage alone.

LEARN

Resilience Is a Skill We Can Develop

Resilience is the capacity to adapt and respond to challenges, stress and difficult experiences. It does not mean that a person never struggles.

Resilience can develop through supportive relationships, problem-solving skills, healthy routines, emotional awareness, positive experiences and access to appropriate support.

Importantly, resilience is not only an individual responsibility. Safe families, supportive schools, inclusive communities and fair social systems can strengthen people's ability to cope.

Healthy Coping Is Not One-Size-Fits-All

Different situations require different strategies. A useful coping strategy should be safe, realistic and appropriate for the situation.

01

Talk

Talk with someone you trust about what you are experiencing.

02

Pause

Give yourself time to breathe, notice your emotions and respond rather than react.

03

Move

Physical activity can support both physical and mental wellbeing.

04

Rest

Adequate sleep and regular routines help support health and emotional regulation.

05

Create

Music, art, writing, hobbies and creativity can provide meaningful ways to express ourselves.

06

Seek Help

When difficulties persist or interfere with daily life, reaching out for professional support is an important step.

Social Connection Is a Health Factor

Humans are social beings. Positive relationships can provide emotional support, belonging, practical help and opportunities to learn from others.

Social connection does not mean being surrounded by people all the time. What matters is having relationships in which people can feel respected, valued and supported.

Think about it:

Who are the people you could talk to if you were having a difficult day?

BREAK THE STIGMA

Mental Health Is Health.

Stigma can make people feel ashamed, isolated or afraid to ask for help. It can also create discrimination and prevent people from accessing appropriate care and support.

Learn Replace myths with evidence.
Listen Take people's experiences seriously.
Respect Never reduce someone to a diagnosis.
Include Build environments where everyone belongs.
HELP-SEEKING

Asking for Help Is a Strength

Everyone needs support sometimes. Reaching out early can be an important part of maintaining wellbeing and addressing problems before they become more difficult.

Start with someone you trust

This might be a parent, family member, friend, teacher, school counsellor, coach or another trusted adult.

Ask a professional

Health professionals and mental health services can help assess concerns and identify appropriate support.

Support a friend

Listen without judging, encourage the person to seek appropriate support and involve a trusted adult when needed.

EQUITY • SOCIAL DETERMINANTS OF HEALTH

Mental Health Is Also About the World Around Us

Not everyone has the same opportunities to achieve good mental health. The conditions in which people are born, grow, learn, work and live can shape both risks and protective factors.

Education

Safe, inclusive and supportive learning environments can influence wellbeing and opportunity.

Income & Resources

Economic security affects access to housing, food, education, recreation and health services.

Housing & Environment

Safe housing and healthy environments are important foundations for wellbeing.

Discrimination

Racism, stigma, exclusion and discrimination can create additional stress and barriers to support.

Access to Care

Geography, cost, availability, culture and language can all influence access to appropriate services.

Community & Belonging

Strong communities can create connection, identity, participation and mutual support.

1. Which statement best describes a protective factor for mental health?

Correct answer: B. Protective factors can strengthen wellbeing and help people cope with challenges.

2. What does resilience mean?

Show answer

Correct answer: B. Resilience involves adapting to challenges and can be strengthened by skills, relationships and supportive environments.

3. Which of the following is an example of positive social connection?

Show answer

Correct answer: B. Respectful listening and supportive relationships can strengthen social connection.

4. Which statement about healthy coping is most accurate?

Correct answer: B. Healthy coping involves safe and appropriate strategies, which can include several forms of support.

Correct answer: B. Stigma can discourage people from talking about mental health and seeking appropriate support.

Show answer

Correct answer: A. SDG 3 is Good Health and Well-being.

9. Which action can help reduce mental health stigma?

A note for young explorers

Learning about mental health can sometimes bring up personal feelings or experiences. If something you are experiencing is worrying you or affecting your daily life, consider talking with a trusted adult or qualified health professional. You do not have to figure everything out by yourself.
