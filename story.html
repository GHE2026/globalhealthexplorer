Every Journey Begins Somewhere

Every meaningful movement begins with an idea. Some begin in classrooms. Others begin in laboratories, communities, or moments of profound reflection.

Global Health Explorers (GHE) began with a simple but ambitious belief: investing in children and young people is one of the most powerful ways to build a healthier, more equitable, and more sustainable world.

Behind this vision are lives dedicated to learning, service, compassion, scientific excellence, and leadership. Their example continues to inspire a new generation to explore, understand, and improve the health of their communities and the wider world.

What is Global Health Explorers?

Global Health Explorers (GHE) is an educational platform dedicated to developing the next generation of global health leaders.

Through evidence-informed learning, data literacy, innovation, leadership development, and meaningful community engagement, GHE empowers children and young people to understand global health challenges and contribute to healthier, more equitable communities.

Guided by its Five Pillars— Education, Data and Innovation, Leadership and Action, Research and Partnerships, and Impact and Sustainability— GHE seeks to inspire young people to become informed citizens, compassionate leaders, responsible innovators, and active contributors to the future of global health.
